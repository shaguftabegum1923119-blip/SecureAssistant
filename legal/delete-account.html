How to Delete Your Account

Secure Assistant · Last updated 24 October 2026 · Contact: secureassistant2319@gmail.com

You can delete your Secure Assistant account and its data in either of these two ways.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion starts straight away. If something fails it is retried automatically until it is complete.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to secureassistant2319@gmail.com

  • Subject: Request to Delete My Account
  • Write your registered mobile number and email ID in the email.

We will permanently delete your account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login details: email, phone number and password.
  • Your profile, cameras, alerts, reports, chats, support tickets, team members and location.
  • All your saved files: photos, videos, vault files, offline clips, face photos and exported reports.
  • Any active auto-renew is cancelled first, so you are not charged again.

What may be kept

Payment and subscription records, because tax and accounting rules require us to keep them.

Only some data

You can also delete single files, faces or chats inside the app without deleting your account.
