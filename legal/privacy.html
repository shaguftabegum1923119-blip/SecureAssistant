Privacy Policy

Secure Assistant · Last updated 22 October 2026 · Contact: secureassistant2319@gmail.com

Secure Assistant is operated by Secure Assistant. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, who helps us run the service, and what choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: first name, last name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account information. Phone number if you use phone sign-in. Address and language if you give them.
  • Location data: only when you press the Location button or allow location, used for map links in an emergency and to set your time zone.
  • Camera settings and health: camera names, connection type, and battery, storage and network status sent by your camera phone.
  • Counts from camera frames: the number of people and vehicles seen. For these frames the image itself is not saved, only the counts.
  • Files you choose to save: video clips, vault files and face photos (only with the consent of the person shown). These are encrypted with a key tied to your account before they are stored.
  • Chat and support: messages and photos you send to our assistant, Abdul Wahab, and your support messages and tickets.
  • Payment information: collected by Razorpay on the website or by Google Play in the Android app, during checkout. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We keep only a record of the plan, amount, date and payment reference.

2. How we use your information

To provide the app features, enable Google sign-in, show location services, show your reports and alerts, give business and security advice, process payments, and give support. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In, Google Play Services and device location services.
  • Google Firebase: sign-in and our database.
  • Backblaze B2: encrypted file storage.
  • Agora: live video connection.
  • Anthropic: your chat messages, and photos you send in chat, are sent to its AI to write the answer.
  • Razorpay: processes website payments. When you pay, your data is shared with Razorpay as per its Privacy Policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy/
  • Google Play Billing: processes payments made inside the Android app.
  • Render: the servers that run our service.

4. Data security

We do not sell your information. Files are encrypted before storage and access to our systems is restricted. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and Google Play, not by us. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your password private.

5. Faces and other people

If you save someone's face photo, you must have that person's consent. You can delete it at any time and it is erased from storage.

6. How long we keep data, and your choices

  • We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account.
  • You can download a copy of your data, edit your details, and delete files or chats inside the app.
  • Payment and subscription records may be kept longer where tax or accounting rules require it.

7. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. How to delete your account

See the Delete account page. In short: use Delete account in the app (Profile), or email us at secureassistant2319@gmail.com.

9. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. Questions: secureassistant2319@gmail.com.
