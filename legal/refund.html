Refund and Cancellation

Secure Assistant · Last updated 24 October 2026 · Contact: secureassistant2319@gmail.com

Cancel auto-renew

You can cancel auto-renew in the app at any time. Your plan then stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and is not charged again.

Refunds

You get a full refund if you were charged twice for the same plan, or if money was deducted and your plan did not start.

If you ask within 7 days of your first purchase and have used the service very little, we may refund it. We decide this case by case.

In other cases a payment is not refundable once the plan period has started.

An approved refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Refund requests are sent from the app (request button in support chat). They are reviewed by us one by one; nothing is refunded automatically and support chat cannot promise a result.

Money deducted but plan not started

Wait a few minutes and retry. If it still did not start, send a support ticket from the app with the date and amount. We will check it.

Purchases made through Google Play

Payments made inside the Android app are handled by Google Play. Their refund and cancellation rules apply and you can manage them in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions.

Extra storage packs

Extra storage lasts 30 days from purchase and does not renew by itself. Buy it again to keep the extra space.
